💰 Budget Tracker

A simple app I made to track my spending — you add what you earn or spend, and it keeps a running total so you always know where your money stands.

Try It

Open budget-tracker.html in your browser. That's it.

What I Made

A budget tracker. You add what you spent or earned, and it shows your running balance plus a little pie chart of where your money's going — food, rent, fun stuff, whatever.

Why I Made It

I'm just starting to learn to code, and I wanted my first project to be something I'd actually use, not just a throwaway exercise.

What You Should Know
No installing, no signing up — just open it in your browser
Your data saves automatically, so it's still there next time you open it
It only remembers stuff on the same device and browser — open it elsewhere and it'll look empty
